# Voices

The opening's voices (round 111) were generated by the user with ElevenLabs text-to-speech
(eleven v4, voices: Adam for Dave, Mark for Marc, Declan Sage for Aaron, Titan for Chris) from
their own script. Made on the ElevenLabs free plan: non-commercial use, with attribution
("Voices made with ElevenLabs", on the title screen's options). A paid plan is needed before
the game is sold.

Cut into one line per file, trimmed and levelled in the session (ffmpeg, -18 LUFS).
