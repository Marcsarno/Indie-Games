# Dungeons of Bones: the story

## The setting: a D&D night

- The whole story is a tabletop game night. **Dave** is the Dungeon Master behind his screen; **Marc**, **Aaron** and **Chris** are the players around the table. **You** are the fifth seat: you pick a miniature (your class) and play.
- **The map on the table is the game.** The camera dives into it to play and lifts back out to the table when a quest is told.
- **Quests are told at the table:** Dave voices whoever gives the quest ("DAVE, AS BROTHER OSRIC"), a friend reacts, you pick your reward, and the camera drops back into the game.
- **Visions** rise from the table while Dave talks: the dead, wolves, bandits, the Skeleton King, Maelis.
- **Small beats don't stop play:** Dave (or a friend) peeks in from the top of the screen with a line, then folds away.
- **You roll the d20** at the start. It always lands on 20.

## Theme and tone

Dark-fantasy dungeon crawl told with a wink. Dave narrates in short, dry lines (THEN THE DEAD GOT UP.); the friends react like players (WOLVES. GREAT. / CALLED IT.). The story is a trail of clues the table pieces together: someone is digging up graves and paying for bones to rebuild an old dead king.

## The story in brief

Oakhollow is gone; all that is left is a camp in the woods (**the Hollow**). The dead walk, and the graves were dug up with spades: someone is paying for bones. You find Aldo the woodcutter caged in a wolf den; he saw a hooded man with a green lantern at the old mine. The road north is blocked by a fallen tree and three dangers; each one leaves a clue (silver stamped M, green ash, a farm's spade). Bandits hold Hollis's farm; their chief has the mine key, a cellar full of graveyard bones, and a letter signed M. Osric names him: **Maelis**, a priest cast out of his order. Under the mine you put the **Skeleton King** back in his grave; Maelis appears, says the King already gave him what he wanted, and leaves south. Act II (Ashford, a mining town) is next.
