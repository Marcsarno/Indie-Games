Bigger Dungeons

Dungeon of Bones, round 38, from your phone notes. The dungeon floors are bigger, with real room variety. There's no fog of war in the view any more, the tents are proper tents, the wolf den has a path in, and leaps no longer get you stuck in walls.

Rooms with room to fight

  • Every floor has one great hall, about 15 by 11 tiles, with a colonnade down its back wall and a brazier in the middle. In the caves it's a great cavern ringed with stalagmites.
  • The rest is a mix of ordinary rooms (7 to 11 tiles across), round caverns (12 to 16 across), crossroads (plus-shaped), long galleries (16 to 20 by 5 or 6), L-shaped rooms, and small side rooms for chests.
  • Corridors into a big room are often 3 tiles wide instead of 2.
  • Bigger rooms hold more monsters, so the number per floor grows with the map (about 30 on the first floor, up to about 100 on the deepest).
  • All the old checks still run on every floor: everything reachable, nothing near the start, champions spread out. A 60-seed sweep of every level passes clean, and floors load as fast as before.

No fog of war in the view

  • Everything on screen is drawn, explored or not. The torches and your own light still decide how bright each part is, with a low ambient so a far room isn't pitch black.
  • The map still fills in as you explore.
  • Monsters still only show where you have a line of sight, so none appear through walls.
  • Adding ?fog=1 to the game's address brings the old fog back, for comparison.

Tents

Two sloped canvas panels on a ridge pole, closed ends, the front door open with one flap folded back, guy ropes and pegs. There were two review passes: in the first, the front pole and rope cut right across the doorway, so the pole went.

Fixes

  • Stuck in walls. The leap, blink and the ranger's leap only checked the single spot they landed on, but the hero's body is wider than that. A landing right beside a wall left it overlapping the wall, and then every move was blocked. All three now only land where the whole body fits, and the hero is nudged out if it ever ends up in a wall. A new test jumps 180 times per class from spots beside walls: 45 to 87 of those got stuck before, none now.
  • Wolf den. A trail runs from the woods road to the den, there's a clearing in front of it, and the woods are a little thinner.
