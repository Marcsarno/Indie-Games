Heroes, Sets and Casts

Dungeon of Bones, round 43: an hour on the three heroes’ art and animation. Armor sets you can recognize at a glance, new cast poses for the mage, and new skill poses for the ranger.

Armor sets

  • Until now a set piece looked exactly like the plain item it was based on. With two or more pieces of a set on, the hero now wears its colors:
    • Warden’s: a white tabard with a gold cross over the plate, a white plume, and a longer white cloak with a gold hem.
    • Scout’s: a red feather in the cap and a green cloak.
    • Magus: a gold star at the hat’s tip and a glowing violet gem at the collar.
    • Stalker’s: a red scarf over the face and a black cloak with a red hem.
  • The ranger’s dark hood was a black blob that read as hair. It’s now slate grey. The Night Hood (the best ranger hood) is now navy, so top gear no longer looks like the Stalker set.
  • Three art passes. In the first, the Warden’s tabard was hidden inside the plate. In the second, the grey hood still looked brown like hair.

The warrior’s best armor

  • Gothic Plate (the warrior’s best chest, level 5) is blackened steel with gold edges on the pauldrons and a spike on each shoulder.
  • The Bascinet (the best helm) has a tall pointed skull and a snouted visor, so the best gear stands out from across a room.

Top-tier items with their own looks

Round 42a added 24 ordinary items that reused older looks. The best ones now have looks of their own: the bastard sword (a longer blade with a swept guard), the morning star (a spiked iron ball), the battle axe (two crescent blades and a top spike), the spangenhelm (a pointed cone with gold ribs and a nasal bar), the runed staff (gold bands, frost motes circling the orb), the sage’s hood (green with a gold hem; in white it read as white hair), drake hide (dark red; rust was too close to leather) and the hunter’s hood (a fur mantle).

Mage casts

  • Frost Nova and Cone of Cold: the staff goes up, then the mage drops into a deep crouch and slams it into the ground, and ice shards spring up at its foot.
  • Fireball: a ball of fire held up in the free hand, then thrown with a long step while the staff stays back.
  • The timings are unchanged; the spell still leaves on the same frame as before.

Ranger skills

  • Evasive Leap used the “stopping” pose. It’s now a crouch, a tucked jump back with the free arm out for balance, and a landing that sits into it.
  • Snare Trap used the bow shot. Now the ranger swings the iron trap back past the hip and tosses it underhand.
  • Multishot used the plain shot. Now the bow is laid over sideways with three arrows on the string, so the fan of arrows reads as a different shot.

Ultimates

  • Arcane Storm: while the storm rages and you stand still, the mage floats a few pixels off the ground, rising and sinking, with the staff held high to one side and the free hand raised, crackling. Walking still works as before.
  • Rain of Arrows: the ranger leans back and looses at the sky, instead of firing a flat shot at the ground ahead. Pointed straight up, the bow vanished behind the head, so it aims about 50° up.
  • The warrior’s Wrath of the Mountain already had its own leap and crash from round 40a.

Blink and the class legendaries

  • Blink now lands in a crouch with the staff and free arm flung out, rising over a quarter of a second.
  • The warrior’s six legendaries each changed his look; the mage’s and ranger’s five didn’t. Now:
    • Staff of Cinders: charred wood with embers in it and a flame over the orb.
    • Seer’s Peak: an open eye on the hat.
    • Mantle of the Magi: stars glinting down the robe’s front.
    • Windrunner: silver limbs and green ribbons at the tips.
    • Wolfhide: a fur collar and a string of wolf teeth.

Dying with some weight

All three heroes now rock back from the killing blow with their arms flung out, drop to their knees, then fall. Before, they tipped straight over. Bandits, who share the hero’s body, die the same way.

Hats and hoods that move

The tip of the mage’s hat and the point of the ranger’s hood now lag behind the head, like the cloaks already did. They stream back and bob with each step, flick forward when you stop, whip round in a spin, toss up on a landing and drift in the Arcane Storm.
