Acts and Arenas

Dungeon of Bones, round 22, step 3 of the plan: the endless loop becomes three acts, each ending in a hand-designed boss arena.

  • Entering a town opens its act with a title card: ACT II, THE BURNING DEEP, ASHFORD.
  • Each act's dungeon keeps one look (crypt, caves, hell). Before, the look changed every two floors.
  • After the Sanctum you're back in Oakhollow as ACT I NIGHTMARE, with everything harder (then HELL, then TORMENT).
  • Bosses now appear only at the end of the wilds and in the final arenas, not on random dungeon floors.

The arenas

Whole-level views. Green: where you arrive. Big red: the boss. Small red: guards.

  • The arenas are drawn as text in the code (one character per tile: floor, pillars, braziers, statues, the boss, guards, eggs and so on), so it's quick to reshape them or add new ones.
  • The generator checks that every floor tile can be reached from the entrance.
