Sharper Props and Skills

Dungeon of Bones, round 27: the last three skills polished, separate impact sounds, and an optional sharper world.

Warrior: the chain hook

  • The chain is drawn at full detail: outlined links that sag while the hook flies and pull straight once it bites.
  • The hook head is barbed. A spark and a clank mark the bite, and dust trails whatever it drags.

Mage: missiles and blink

  • Missiles: drawn at full detail with a ribbon trail along the curving path they home in on, and a small flash and zap when they hit.
  • Blink: a ring of arcane light where you leave and where you land, a streak between the two, and a deeper whoosh.

Sounds

Each impact now layers its own sound over the shared boom: a stone crack for the slam, leap and meteor, a fire sizzle for explosions, an ice clink for the cone, a chain rattle and a metal clank for the hook, and a zap for missiles.

Sharper world (your call)

Trees, buildings, braziers, barrels and other props can now be drawn at twice the detail, like the characters. Floors and walls stay as they are. It's off by default. On the title screen, DETAIL: CLASSIC or SHARP switches it, and your choice is remembered.
