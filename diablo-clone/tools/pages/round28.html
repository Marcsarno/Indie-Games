Warm Light and Glow

Dungeon of Bones, round 28: torchlight leans warm, shadows lean cool, bright things glow, and characters catch the light and cast shadows away from it.

What changed

  • Warm light, cool shadows. Wherever torches, braziers or your own light brighten a spot, the colors lean warm orange. As the light falls off into the dark, they lean blue. Black outlines and plain daylight stay exactly as drawn.
  • Glow. A soft, stepped halo hangs in the air around flames, braziers, campfires, night lanterns, spells in flight, explosions and the town portal. It spills over walls and characters, so bright things feel bright.
  • Cost: none worth measuring (a dungeon at 4x slower CPU runs 34.9 ms per frame vs 34.0 before).
  • It's on by default. LIGHT: OLD on the title screen turns both off.

Blind test

Rim light and torch shadows

  • Rim light. Each character finds the strongest light reaching it: a torch, brazier, campfire or lantern, or your own light for monsters near you. The edge of the character facing that light gets a warm highlight, 2 pixels wide when the light is close.
  • Torch shadows. Each character also casts a shadow stretched away from that light, longer when the light is close and fading toward its tip. It swings around them as they move past a fire.
  • Outdoors in daylight, neither applies, since the sun is overhead. At night they do.
  • Cost: about 2 ms per frame at 4x slower CPU, nothing measurable at normal speed. LIGHT: OLD on the title turns these off along with the warm light and glow.
