Bag, Guides and Previews

Dungeon of Bones, round 45: quality-of-life changes. The music keeps playing in menus, the bag shows upgrades at a glance, the first new weapon and the first skill modifier are walked through once, and the skills screen plays each skill live before you pick it.

The bag

  • Tabs: GEAR and SKILLS at the top, so the skills screen is easy to find. A tab shows a mark when it has something new: a green arrow for an upgrade, a purple dot for a new skill modifier.
  • Markers on bag items: a big green ▲ when the item beats what you wear, a red ▼ when it’s worse, and NEW on items picked up since you last opened the bag. Gear for another class is greyed out.
  • Tapping an item: the slot it goes in glows. A card shows the verdict in large letters (UPGRADE, WORSE, ABOUT THE SAME or EMPTY SLOT), the item beside the one you wear, and what changes if you wear it, in green and red.
  • Faster equipping: double-tap an item to wear it. WEAR BEST lights up when the bag holds any upgrade and wears the best item for each slot.

The first new weapon, walked through

  • The second wolf you kill (or your eighth kill of anything, at level 4 or below) always drops a weapon for your class that beats your starting one.
  • Picking it up slows the game to a stop and dims everything except the bag button, which gets a bouncing arrow. Only the bag button works until you open it.
  • In the bag the arrow moves to the new weapon, then to EQUIP. When it’s on, a line shows your damage before and after.
  • This happens once per hero and is saved. Old saves past level 4 never see it.

The first skill modifier, walked through

  • Every class opens its first modifier at level 3, and that level-up stops the game the same way.
  • The arrow then leads to the bag, the SKILLS tab and the new modifier, where its preview plays, and then to CHOOSE.
  • Later modifiers don’t stop the game. You just get the purple badge and the tab dot.

The skills screen, with live previews

  • Tap a skill button, then the plain skill or any of its three modifiers. The window above plays it on three practice dummies, on a loop, with the damage numbers. Locked modifiers can be watched too; they show the level they open at.
  • Each choice has a longer explanation of what it does and when to pick it. The screen also shows its cost and cooldown, and the damage one loop deals with your current gear.
  • It’s the real skill code, not a recording: a second, sealed-off copy of the game runs in the background with your hero, gear and level, in a small lit practice yard. That copy never saves, plays no sound, and can’t touch your game. All 52 skills and modifiers across the three classes play in it without errors.
  • Speed: on a desktop CPU slowed to a quarter, the skills screen takes about the same time per frame as normal play (34 to 48 ms against 26 to 38 ms), because the game world behind the bag and skills screens is no longer drawn.

Music

The song no longer stops when dialogue, the bag or the skills screen opens. It keeps playing about 40% quieter, dipping and coming back smoothly.
