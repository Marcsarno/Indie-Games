Monsters at Home

Dungeon of Bones, round 36. Before they notice you, monsters go about their business: some wander, some walk a patrol route through several rooms, packs move together behind a leader, and some guard a spot. They also only notice you when you're in front of them, or very close.

What they do over 30 seconds

The routines

  • Packs: monsters of one kind standing together form a pack with a leader. The rest keep a loose formation beside and behind it, and catch up if they fall behind.
  • Wander: strolls between spots near home (within about 3.5 tiles) and pauses at each to look around.
  • Patrol: walks a loop through two or three rooms (or clearings outdoors) along the corridors, pausing at each stop. Skeletons and archers patrol most.
  • Guard: stays put, looks around, shifts about. Monsters around a campfire always guard.
  • Each family has its own leaning: spiders mostly wander, hounds and wolves roam as packs, skeletons patrol, and fallen do a bit of everything.
  • No route ends near the level's start, and nothing wanders within 13 tiles of the Oakhollow camp.

Being noticed

Before, any monster saw you all around out to 7 tiles. Now each one sees out to 7.5 tiles in front, in a wide arc (about 150 degrees), but only 3 tiles behind. A patrol walking away from you won't turn round, and a guard looking about might spot you. You can slip past, or pick your moment.
