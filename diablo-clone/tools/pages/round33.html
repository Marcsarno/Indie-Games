The Ranger

Dungeon of Bones, round 33: a third class. A hooded archer with four skills, three modifiers for each, an ultimate and its own gear.

The look

Drawn the same way as the other heroes, with two review passes. The first had a big cowl that hid the jerkin, and the bow at rest was a thin line behind the leg. The second shrank the cowl, held the bow out from the body, and made the quiver larger, with fletchings showing over the shoulder.

Its resource is called focus. It's steadier than the warrior's fury and smaller than the mage's mana, and it refills on its own. Health sits between the two other classes.

Note: "Evasive Roll" became Evasive Leap. The character rig can't tumble yet, so it's a quick backward hop with dust and arrows on landing. A real roll needs new rig work, which I can do in a later pass.

Gear and the title screen

  • Bows: Hunting Bow, Longbow, Horn Bow. Chest: Leather Jerkin, Studded Jerkin, Hide Coat. Hoods: Ranger Hood, Shadow Hood. Quivers go in the shield slot: Quiver and War Quiver.
  • Two legendaries: Windrunner (a longbow: +30% attack speed, +15% move speed) and Wolfhide (a hide coat: +30 life, +10% crit). One set: Stalker's (cowl, hide and quiver).
  • A ranger only finds ranger gear, as the other classes only find theirs. The warrior's old "Ranger's" set is now called Scout's, to avoid a mix-up.

Tested

A new test fires every skill and modifier at test dummies:

  • Every trap snaps and roots the monsters in reach, and a rooted monster doesn't move.
  • The leap takes no damage in the air.
  • The mark picks the elite over a weak monster, and the elite takes 35% more damage (70% with Death Mark). Spreading Mark passes on when the target dies.
  • Rain of Arrows hits the whole group of seven.
  • All 300 test drops were ranger gear.

The full run through every level passes for all three classes, and all the earlier tests still pass.
