  • Every third town and forest is at night: dim moonlight, the hero carries a light, and lanterns, campfires and cabin windows glow.
  • Two item sets in green, Warden's (visor, plate, wall) and Ranger's (hood, jerkin, targe): two pieces give a bonus, all three a bigger one.
  • Shrines of Frenzy (attack speed), Stone (half damage) and Fortune (a shower of gold) in the forests; each works once.
  • Mana potions (blue) drop with healing potions and are drunk on pickup.
  • A new test plays levels 1 to 12 (every area and boss) and checks exits, bosses and saves: all pass.

How it plays

  • Town, forest, then three dungeon floors with the broodmother at the bottom. After her, a new town and an autumn forest, and so on.
  • In town, tap attack next to the merchant to buy a potion for 25 gold, or next to the smith to sell everything in your bag that you are not wearing.
  • Outdoors it is daylight and there is no fog. Trees go see-through when you walk behind them.
