What changed

  • The wind-up before the blow is shorter, so the hit lands close to your tap. The swings use the same poses; only the hold times changed. Enemies keep their slower wind-ups, so you can still see their attacks coming.
  • After the blow lands, pushing the stick cancels the rest of the swing. Holding attack (or tapping again) starts the next swing after the follow-through instead of waiting for the full recovery.
  • A tap during a swing is remembered for a moment and plays as soon as he's free, so a combo never drops a tap.
  • If the target is slightly out of reach (for example, after being knocked back), he steps in during the wind-up instead of walking over first.
  • Every hit makes the enemy flinch, unless it's already attacking. Axe blows, combo finishers and big hits also interrupt its attack. Elites only flinch from finishers, and bosses don't flinch at all.
  • The freeze on a hit scales with the blow: short for a sword, longer for an axe or a finisher, longest for a kill. The hero's freeze is shorter than the enemy's, so the controls stay snappy.
  • Each hit shoves harder: the sword about 0.4 tile, the axe about 0.6, finishers about 0.8. Elites are pushed half as far and bosses hardly at all.

Side effect

Holding attack now does about 50% more damage per second, and enemies interrupted by heavy blows hit back less often, so fights are easier. Step F of the plan (enemy health and pack sizes) is where that gets rebalanced.
