Warrior Skills

Dungeon of Bones, round 24: a new kit for the warrior. Whirlwind stays; three new skills; mana now fills by fighting.

The four buttons

Level 1: Ground Slam
An overhead chop straight into the floor (no jump). The ground splits in a cone in front of him, about 4 tiles long: glowing fissures, rocks and dust. Everything in the cone takes 1.5 times weapon damage as a heavy blow (big knockback, through shields) and is dazed for 0.7 seconds. He aims at the nearest enemy. 8 mana, every 2.5 seconds.
Level 2: Chain Hook (my suggestion)
He throws a chain at the nearest enemy within 7 tiles. It drags that enemy, and anything standing right beside it, to his feet, dazed. A boss, elite or champion won't budge: the warrior is hauled to it instead, so it doubles as a gap-closer. Hook a pack in, then slam it. 6 mana, every 4 seconds (no mana spent if nothing's in reach).
Level 3: Whirlwind
Unchanged: he spins, hitting everything in reach four times.
Level 4: Leap
He launches himself in an arc at the nearest enemy 2 to 7 tiles away (or, with none, up to 6 tiles toward where you push the stick). Nothing can hit him in the air. The landing smashes everything within about 2 tiles for 1.8 times weapon damage with a finisher's knockback, dazes them and cracks the floor. Good for getting in, getting out, and crossing the room. 10 mana, every 5 seconds.

Fury

Each enemy his swings hit gives back 1.5 mana (up to 3 enemies per swing), so a warrior fills his mana by fighting instead of waiting for it, about 10 mana a second while hitting two enemies. Shield Bash, War Cry and Holy Bolt are gone.

Other ideas for level 2

If the hook isn't it: Berserk (a few seconds of red-eyed rage: faster swings and life steal) or Battle Cry (stuns and taunts everything around him). Either is a quick swap.
