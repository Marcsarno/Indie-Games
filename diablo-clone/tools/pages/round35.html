Monster Movement

Dungeon of Bones, round 35, step 2 of making monsters fun to fight. They notice you, take turns, circle, stalk and burst, and look alive before they've seen you.

Groups coming at you

What changed

  • Noticing. The first monster to see you hops, shows a '!' and growls. The others nearby turn and join a beat later each, so the room comes alive in a ripple instead of all at once.
  • Taking turns. In a pack, only two press the attack at a time. The rest circle you about two and a half tiles out, sidling with their eyes on you. After striking, a pack monster backs off for a moment and gives up its turn, so they rotate in and out. A pair, or one alone, just comes at you.
  • Stalk and burst. Pack monsters and hounds move in a rhythm: a slow stalk, then a quick burst forward.
    • Hounds and wolves weave from side to side as they come.
    • Skeletons come on slow and steady.
    • Spiders skitter in short dashes with stops between.
  • Breaking off. When a small monster drops below 30% life, there's a 40% chance it breaks off and runs for about a second (it only happens once per monster).
  • Before they see you. Monsters waiting in a room look around and shuffle about near where they stand, instead of standing frozen.

Bosses, archers and casters are unchanged, and COMBAT: OLD on the title screen turns all of this off for comparison.
