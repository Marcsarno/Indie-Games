New Button Layout

Dungeon of Bones, round 32: the health and mana orbs become two thin bars at the bottom left. The attack button takes the bottom-right corner, with the skills fanned round it the way Diablo Immortal does it.

Warrior

Mage

What changed

  • Attack is in the bottom-right corner and slightly bigger. Tapping anywhere on the lower right still attacks, as before.
  • The four skills sit on one arc around it, evenly spaced from its left round to straight above it. The ultimate sits just outside the arc, between the second and third skills. The potion sits at the arc's foot.
  • Health and mana are thin bars at the bottom left with the numbers at their ends. They're red and blue, or red and orange for the warrior's fury, with your level above them. The XP bar is a hairline along the very bottom.
  • The movement stick's resting spot moved down into the space the health orb left.
  • Buff timers (war cry and the rest) now show above the buttons instead of over the bars.
