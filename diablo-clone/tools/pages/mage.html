The Mage

Dungeon of Bones, round 18: a second class with a robe, a wizard hat, a staff and four spells.

On the title screen, pick MAGE and tap New Game. The warrior and the mage keep separate saves, so your warrior's progress is untouched.

The spells in a fight

Spells

ButtonWhat it does
Attack: Magic MissilesTwo homing arcane darts per cast (three from level 5). Free to cast; hold to keep firing. Attack speed and the frenzy shrine make it faster.
Fireball (level 1)A large fireball that explodes on the first thing it touches, hitting everything nearby and knocking enemies back. 9 mana.
Frost Nova (level 2)A ring of frost bursts from the mage and freezes everything close by for about 3 seconds. Frozen enemies can't move or attack, take 40% more damage, and shatter when killed. Elites stay frozen 2 seconds and bosses 1. 14 mana.
Meteor (level 3)Called down on the biggest group in sight. A ring marks where it will land, then it streaks in and crashes in a wide disk of fire with a big screen shake. 22 mana.

All spell damage comes from the staff, so a better staff makes every spell hit harder. Pushing the stick cancels the end of a cast, so you can cast and then run.

Look

  • Built on the warrior's body: the same face, hair, walk and proportions.
  • The robe is a long skirt whose hem follows the feet as he walks, with bell sleeves, a gold stripe and a gold band near the hem. No cloak over the robe.
  • The wizard hat sits high and tipped back so the eyes still show; its point bends backward.
  • The staff is carried upright and leaning out, so it doesn't cross the face. When casting, it's thrust up toward the target.

Screens

  • The mage has less life than the warrior (32 vs 40 at level 1) but more than twice the mana, and mana refills more than 3 times as fast.
  • A mage only finds mage gear: three staves, three robes, the wizard hat and the hood. There are also three mage legendaries (Staff of Cinders, Mantle of the Magi, Seer's Peak) and a three-piece Magus set.
