Stairs, Portal and Skill Polish

Dungeon of Bones, round 25: fewer junk drops, stairs you can't miss, a town portal, and bigger impacts on the warrior's and mage's skills.

Fewer, better drops

Grey items no longer drop. Items fall on 8% of kills instead of 14%, and the grey drops became small piles of gold. Rares stay at about the same rate, so the bag fills with things worth looking at.

Stairs instead of a hole

Town portal

  • It's free and you can use it any number of times. Opening it takes half a second.
  • Coming back puts you on the same spot. Dead monsters stay dead, open chests stay open, loot stays on the floor and the map stays explored.
  • You arrive in town by the well, next to the merchant and smith.
  • If you quit while in town, the gate is still there when you load. The dungeon comes back fresh, as a reloaded save always has.

Warrior skills

  • Ground slam: a white flash where the axe bites, a wave of dust rolling out across the cone, stone chunks thrown up. The cracks now stay in the floor.
  • Whirlwind: a steel smear spins around him (in front of and behind his body), and dust swirls at his feet.
  • Leap: the landing flashes white, sends out a ring of dust and stones, and leaves cracks behind.

Mage spells

  • Fireball and meteor: a white first frame, then a ring of fire rolling to the blast's edge. The meteor also throws stones and leaves a cracked crater.
  • Cone of cold: a bright frost front sweeps out, and a sheet of frost with glints stays on the ground for about 3 seconds.

Skill buttons

A button pulses with a white rim when its skill fires. It flashes when the cooldown ends, and shakes with a red rim if you don't have the mana.
