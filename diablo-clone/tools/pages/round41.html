The New Camp

Dungeon of Bones, round 41, from your notes: monsters were too close to the camp, the camp was too small and crowded, and the character screen made it hard to tell which hero you were picking.

A walled camp with room

  • A wall of sharpened logs rings the camp, about three times the old camp’s width, with a gate toward each quarter: the mine to the north, the farm to the east, the woods to the west, the road south. Each gate has a lantern on either side.
  • No monster within 26 tiles of the fire, about two screens away, so none are in view from camp. A test checks this on 50 different maps, along with everything still being reachable.
  • Everyone has their own place round the fire and its log benches: Brother Osric at a stone altar with candles and a gold cross on the cathedral steps, the smith at his forge with iron and barrels, the potion seller at a covered wagon with a shelf of glowing bottles and a stall, the refugees’ tents in a row by the well.
  • Two places wait for people the quests will bring back: the woodcutter’s chopping stump with the axe in it and a stack of logs, and the farmer’s hay cart. They’re empty for now; the quest chain fills them.
  • The graveyard is outside the wall, behind the cathedral. The farm, the mine, the woods and the den moved out with the bigger map.
  • Speed: the same frame time in camp as before. The hub takes about a quarter of a second longer to load on a desktop (the map is 1.7 times bigger).

The character screen

  • One card per class with its hero drawn big, turning slowly, how it plays in two lines, its four skills, and its saved level.
  • Tap a card to pick it: a gold frame, the hero turns to you and swings, its skill icons light up. It remembers the last class you played.
  • One big button starts: CONTINUE AS ... when that class has a save, else NEW GAME AS .... START OVER (only shown with a save) asks for a second tap before it replaces the save.
  • The developer settings (combat, detail, light) are folded away under OPTIONS.
