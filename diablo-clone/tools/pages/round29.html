Hits That Land

Dungeon of Bones, round 29 (plan step 1): a new magic missile, monsters that react to what hits them, deaths that match the killing blow, and visible wind-ups.

Mage

  • Magic missile: one heavier arcane bolt per cast instead of a fan of thin darts. It's a fat glowing orb with three sparks circling it and a crackling tail. It flies a third slower (5 tiles a second, no speeding up) and turns more gently. Each hit pushes the target back and makes it flinch. Damage per second is about the same.
  • Fireball and meteor set monsters alight: they glow and flicker orange, burn for 2.4 seconds, and small ones may panic. Monsters killed by fire burn away to ash and leave a scorch mark.
  • Cone of cold: monsters killed by cold shatter into ice.
  • Wind-ups: the fireball and cone are gathered a beat before they leave the staff, and the meteor holds the staff up longest. The missile stays snappy.

Warrior

  • Slam and leap knock ordinary monsters to the ground: they fall, lie there for a moment and get back up. A kill with a heavy blow throws the body back in an arc.
  • Hook: the caught pack is yanked off its feet and bounces as it's dragged in.
  • Whirlwind: each turn staggers what it hits backward, with a low whoosh under the swish.
  • Bosses and elites don't get knocked down or thrown.

Skill modifiers (plan step 2, first two skills)
