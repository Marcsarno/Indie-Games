Oakhollow Opening Area

Dungeon of Bones, round 26: a hand-built start around a cathedral, quests from the priest, two side areas, the act's ending, and sturdier level generation underneath.

The map

The camp at the cathedral

  • Brother Osric stands on the steps. A gold ! means he has quests for you, and a ? means one is ready to hand in.
  • Smithy buys your bag loot. Potions sells healing potions for 25 gold.

The four directions

  • North, the old mine: diggers, a new creature (hunched, snouted, in a dented iron cap, swinging a pick, with a lamp at the belt). The mine leads straight to the first dungeon floor.
  • East, the farm: a farmhouse, a windmill and fields held by bandits. The farmhouse door leads down to the cellar.
  • West, the woods: they get denser toward the wolf den, a rock mound you can go into.
  • South, the gate: shut until the act's boss is dead.

  • He gives all three the first time you talk to him. Each one pays gold, experience and a rare-or-better item when you hand it in.
  • The den and the cellar are short generated floors of five rooms. The leader waits in the farthest room, and the way out is beside the entrance.
  • When you come back out, you're at the door you went in by, and the opening area is just as you left it.

The end of Act I

Act I's old forest level is skipped, since the woods now live in the opening area. Acts II and III are unchanged.

Under the hood: steadier levels

  • Every finished level is checked with the hero's real size: the stairs, every chest and every monster have to be reachable. If a check fails, the level is rebuilt from the next seed.
  • Monsters get harder with walking distance from the start, and none stand within 7 steps of it. Packs only go where all their members fit.
  • Champions are capped and kept at least 12 steps apart, in side rooms first. Chests go in dead ends and side rooms.
  • Doorways and the approach to the stairs are kept clear of props. Up to two loops are added, so fewer routes dead-end.
  • A test runs 300 seeds of every level type. All of them generate, with no unreachable or stuck monsters.
  • Loot: grey items drop a quarter as often as before. Blue and yellow are back at their original rates.
