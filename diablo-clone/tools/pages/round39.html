Elite Powers

Dungeon of Bones, round 39. Champions are now elites: each leads a pack and has its own powers. Every power warns you before it hurts and can be beaten, and each one has its own death.

Champion fights, before and now

  • Every elite leads a pack. It takes up to three monsters of its kind near it. A lone elite gets two at its side, so every elite fight is a pack fight. When the leader dies, the pack falters for a moment.
  • Followers get a weaker copy of the leader's first power (a shorter fire trail, a smaller shield) and 30% more health, but no name.
  • How many powers: one in Act I, two from Act II, three on Nightmare. The first floors only use the four simplest powers (burning, shielded, vampiric, summoner). Frozen and blinking appear from the second crypt floor on.
  • The first time you meet a power, a line names it and says how to beat it, for example "BURNING: STAY OUT OF THE FLAMES".
  • Labels: only the nearest elite shows its name and powers. The others get a small colored dot per power over their heads, so names never pile up.
  • Quest leaders: the Alpha is BLINKING and the Bandit Chief is BURNING.
  • Rewards: each extra power adds loot and experience.

An elite is a little more dangerous than an old champion, but nothing kills you in a few seconds. The real difference comes from what each power asks of you. The new test also checks every power and its counter: you only burn while standing in a lit flame; a broken crystal does nothing; one heavy blow breaks the shield and staggers; a vampire you step away from heals nothing; a summoner's minions all crumble with it; a blinker lands beside you and takes 30% more while it stands open; banned pairs never roll together in 2,000 tries.

How the effects were reviewed

Three passes, judged against the rubric on a dark crypt floor and on bright grass, with frames side by side to catch flicker. Pass 2 replaced the glitter with scorched ground and real flames (the same flames as the torches). It gave the shield a dark rim, a bright band lit from the upper left and a light veil, and made the frost crystal a floating ice diamond with an outline. The blink became a white-cored violet column, and the summoning circle got a dark edge and glowing posts. Pass 3 made the vampire's drops twice as big, with a lower arc. Each new pass was picked over the last in both left/right orders.
