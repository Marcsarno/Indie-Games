Ultimates

Dungeon of Bones, round 31: a fifth button from level 10, with one big move per class. It charges as you fight, not on a timer.

The button

  • It sits left of the other skill buttons and is locked (LV10) until level 10. On a keyboard it's 5 or R.
  • Damage you deal charges it: a monster's worth of damage gives 4%, and each kill gives 1% more. Champions and elites give 6% more, and bosses 20%. That's about 20 ordinary monsters, so one or two big fights.
  • The ultimate's own damage doesn't charge it. When it's full, a message says so and a sound plays. The charge is saved with your game.

Warrior: Wrath of the Mountain

  • He leaps straight up, hangs for a moment gathering light, and drives down. Nothing can touch him in the air.
  • A ring of ground 5 tiles across shatters and stays cracked. Monsters near the middle take up to 6× his weapon damage.
  • Everything but bosses is knocked down for 1.6 s and pushed back. Bosses are dazed.
  • Then 16 rocks rain over the area for about a second and a half. A shadow on the ground shows where each will land.

Mage: Arcane Storm

  • For 4 seconds, arcane lightning strikes monsters within 9 tiles, about 36 bolts in all. Each one deals 1.4× her staff damage and pushes.
  • It goes for whatever has been struck least, so the storm spreads over everything on screen. With nothing in sight, bolts fall around her anyway.
  • She can keep walking (slower) but casts nothing else while it rages.

Also fixed

During a leap, the view stopped refreshing, so the world dimmed a little in mid-air. It now stays lit (the ultimate's longer jump made this obvious).
