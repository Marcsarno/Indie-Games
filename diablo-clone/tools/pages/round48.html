Arrow, Light and Hollow

Dungeon of Bones, round 48: the three things saved before the break. The quest arrow is always on and leads step by step, the dungeons get new light, and the Act I camp is replaced by The Hollow from the approved layout.

1. The quest arrow, always on

  • There's no distance limit anymore. It always shows the next thing that moves the story on: the person to talk to, the Alpha, Aldo's cage, the nearest bandit, the cellar door, the stairs down, the King himself.
  • It follows the ground, not a straight line. It bends around walls, trees and houses the way you'd actually walk.
  • If you're somewhere that isn't this step's place (in the cellar while you should be in the den, or in the Riverlands while your next step is in camp), it points you to the way out first.
  • When the goal is on screen, a small arrow bobs over it. People who already show a ! or ? don't get one.
  • Tested by walking only where the arrow points: it reached the goal at all 24 checkpoints (every story step, the den, the cellar, both mine floors, the King's hall).

2. Dungeon light

Before on the left, now on the right. Brightness wasn't the real problem; the light flattened the shading. Near a torch, the game slid every color up its ramp until the top shades merged into one flat color.

  • Lit areas brighten and warm up instead of losing their shades, and the brightest spots roll off gently.
  • Dark areas have a floor, so nothing on screen is pure black, and they fall off more gently.
  • Torch pools are wider with a softer edge.
  • Shadows from shape: the floor darkens toward walls, into corners and round the feet of props, and walls darken toward the ground.
  • Compare while you play: open the map in any dungeon and tap LIGHT: NEW / OLD. It's also in OPTIONS on the title screen.
  • Outdoors is unchanged. Frame time is the same; each floor takes 8 to 60 ms longer to load.

3. The Hollow

  • Always going up: the camp sits at the bottom. Roads climb to every clearing through open woods with clumps of trees, and thick forest forms the walls between areas.
  • Monster levels by area: camp safe; the brook meadow and the glade 1 to 2; Westwood and the tower yard 2 to 3; the graveyard path 3. The barrow is danger ground at level 6.
  • The brook (with bridges), the den cliffs and the north crags, the standing stones, the graves, and four treasure caches.
  • The fallen tree lies across the road north, with the forest closed right up to it, so it's the only way on. Walk up to it and the game says only a woodcutter could move it. Once Aldo is home it lies cut at the roadside. Tested on 50 seeds: no way round it while it's down.
  • Three doors: the wolf den (northeast, past the glade); the barrow (level 6: skeletons, archers, ghoul hounds and a wraith; one floor for now); the old watchtower (goblins and a chieftain, level 3).
  • Directions in the dialogue now match the map (the den is northeast; the farm and the mine are north, past the tree).

The Riverlands stand-in: the farm, Hollis's bandits, the cellar and the mine aren't on the Hollow layout, because they belong to region 2. So the story still works until region 2 is drawn, the road north past the tree leads to a small stand-in: the farm and its cellar, the chained mine, the road on to Act II (shut until the King falls), and the road back south. It gets replaced when the Riverlands art and layout come in.

  • The whole story runs start to finish on the new maps, and every door, person and clearing can be reached from camp on 50 seeds.
  • Walking to every clearing and door from the camp gate gets there 15 of 15 times with no time stuck. On the way I fixed two movement snags: bodies now slide off the corners of the forest's edge, and when pressed into a corner at a shallow angle they slide sideways instead of stopping.
  • Loading the Hollow takes about 18% longer than the old camp did, for a map 42% bigger.
