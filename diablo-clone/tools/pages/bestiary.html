Bestiary

Every monster, hero outfit, gear look and item in Dungeon of Bones, exactly as the game draws them. For an image generator or artist to design new ones in the same style.

How to use it

  • Give the generator an overview sheet (heroes or monsters) plus the palette as the style reference, and describe the new design in the prompt below.
  • For new armor, also give it that class’s gear row, so it sees how a piece sits on the hero.
  • Bring the result back to the art chat. It rebuilds the design from the game’s 3D shapes and runs it through the art review, so ask for clear silhouettes, colors and shapes rather than fine painted detail.

The style

  • View: isometric 3/4 view from above, with 2:1 diamond floor tiles (32×16 game pixels). Characters have 8 facings.
  • Size: the screen is 270×480 game pixels. A hero is about 39 game pixels tall. Characters are drawn at twice that detail (one sprite pixel is half a game pixel), which is what these sheets show.
  • Proportions: chibi. A big round head, about a third of the height, on a short, sturdy body. Big readable weapons and helmets.
  • Construction: every part is a simple 3D shape (spheres, cylinders, boxes, cones) shaded in flat bands of 3 to 5 tones from one color ramp. No gradients, no texture noise, no anti-aliasing.
  • Light: from the upper left, with darker, slightly cooler shadow sides at the lower right.
  • Outline: a 1-pixel dark outline around the silhouette, and no black lines inside it.
  • Colors: only the palette below. Each material (bone, iron, cloth, leather, skin, fur) has its own ramp.
  • Animation: idle, a walk cycle, attack wind-up and strike, hit and death. The sheets show one frame of each.

Prompt template

Pixel art game sprite, same style as the attached reference sheet: chibi proportions (big round head, short sturdy body), isometric 3/4 top-down view, shown from 4 angles (front, front 3/4, side, back) side by side. Built from simple chunky shapes with flat 3-5 tone shading, light from the upper left, a 1-pixel dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, limited palette (attached), plain dark background, about 80 pixels tall.
Subject: [describe the new monster / armor / weapon here].
