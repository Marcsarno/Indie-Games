Round 15: look, run and swings

Three-hit combo and skill poses

Attacks in the game

Each swing in place

  • The shoulders turned the wrong way: forward on the wind-up, back on the strike. That is what made the wind-up look like a hug. Now they coil back and unwind through the hit. The skeleton had the same bug.
  • The sword was held straight out from the arm the whole time, which looked like a spear jab. Now the wrist lays the blade back in the wind-up and rolls it over in the follow-through, the elbow bends and then extends, and the slash cuts diagonally across the body.
  • He steps in with the front foot and drops his weight on the hit, with a small lunge of about a sixth of a tile. Walls stop it, and it never pushes him into an enemy.
  • Axes, maces, flails and greatswords do an overhead chop: he rises onto his toes, then crouches into the hit and holds it. Without a shield he uses both hands.
  • Swing again right away and he comes back with a backhand, so a string of hits alternates instead of repeating. Timing and damage are unchanged.
  • The shield arm reaches forward in the wind-up and pulls back on the strike.

Walk in the game

Walk cycle in place

What changed in the walk

  • The arms were swinging with the leg on the same side, like pacing. They now swing opposite the legs, and the shoulders turn against the hips.
  • The feet slid about 5 pixels every step. Now the planted foot stays put on the floor, because the cycle advances with the distance actually covered.
  • The hero moves at jogging speed, so his cycle is a jog: a short moment in the air, a dip as each foot lands, the heel kicking up behind.
  • The hips shift over the planted foot. A free off hand pumps, and a shield only rocks.
  • A push-off pose when he starts and a planted stop pose when he stops, each under a tenth of a second.
  • Skeletons and fallen got the same fixes with their own timing: the skeleton walks, the fallen scurries.
