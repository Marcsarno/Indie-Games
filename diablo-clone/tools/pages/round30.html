Skill Modifiers

Dungeon of Bones, round 30: the last seven skills get their three modifiers, picked on the same skills screen as the fireball and slam ones. Each skill unlocks its modifiers 2, 4 and 6 levels after the skill itself.

Where to pick them

Magic missile (your attack)

  • Seekers: 3 small bolts in a fan. Each goes after a different monster, so it's good against packs.
  • Piercing Bolt: A fast, straight lance of light that goes through every monster in its line.
  • Arcane Orb: A slow, big orb. Every 0.4 s it zaps up to four monsters near it, then it bursts. The cast is slower.

Cone of cold

  • Frost Nova: A ring of frost all around you, shorter reach.
  • Ice Shards: A long, narrow cone (6 tiles) that hits almost twice as hard.
  • Deep Freeze: Freezes for 5 s instead of 3. A frozen monster you kill bursts and freezes the ones beside it.

Meteor

  • Shower: Three smaller meteors land one after another around the target. Same total damage, spread wider.
  • Molten Crater: The crater burns for 4 seconds.
  • Comet: A ball of ice instead of fire: it freezes the monsters it hits instead of burning them.

Blink

  • Arcane Burst: A blast where you land that throws monsters back.
  • Double Blink: The button stays ready for 1.5 s after a blink, and the second blink is free.
  • Frost Step: Freezes whatever stood near you when you left.

Chain hook

  • Long Chain: Reaches 9.5 tiles instead of 7, and drags in every monster along the chain.
  • Barbed Hook: Hits harder, and the catch stays dazed twice as long.
  • Tether: Pulls you to whatever it hits, even small monsters, for a heavy blow that knocks things down.

Whirlwind

  • Dust Devils: Small whirlwinds spin off you and drift into nearby monsters.
  • Long Spin: Spins twice as long and drags monsters in toward the blade. Costs 6 more fury.
  • Blade Storm: A wider circle and faster, lighter hits.

Leap

  • Burning Land: A ring of fire on landing, and the ground keeps burning.
  • Earthquake: A much wider landing. Everything but bosses is knocked down.
  • Pounce: Jumps up to 10 tiles. Lands one huge blow on the target (3.2× instead of 1.8×).

Balance

Each modifier was tested on dummies, with crits off, against the plain skill. Most deal about the plain total in a different shape: wider, longer or on more monsters. The orb and Long Spin deal more in a crowd, but the orb casts slower and Long Spin costs more fury. Pounce trades area for one big hit. All the earlier tests and both full runs (warrior and mage) still pass.
