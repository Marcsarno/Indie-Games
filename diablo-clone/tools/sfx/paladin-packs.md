# Paladin SFX packs (round 2)

All downloaded from opengameart.org (archive.org was searched too, but nothing usable turned up: most CC audio there is NC/ND music, and the one "CC0 church bell" item is an unattributed re-upload of a Pixabay file, so I skipped it).
Every path below is relative to `sfx-pal/`. Durations come from ffprobe. "takes" means the number of separate hits or whooshes that silencedetect found in the file, so it needs slicing.
I picked these from filenames, descriptions and durations only. **Nothing has been listened to yet.**

License key: CC0 = no attribution needed. CC-BY / OGA-BY = credit the author. CC-BY-SA = credit the author, and edits of the sound must be shared under the same license.
