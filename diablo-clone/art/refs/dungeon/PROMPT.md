# Crypt face-lift: prompts for concept art (round 68, revised)

Facts from the game (checked against the screenshots): the screen is 270x420 game pixels,
shown at 2x (540x840, 9:14); a floor tile is a 32x16 diamond; the hero is about 39 px tall
(about 1/11 of the screen's height); a tile is about as wide as the hero is tall; about 8 tiles
span the screen. Light comes from the upper left. The palette is limited and objects have 1 px dark outlines.

Use: attach the three screenshots in `current/`, paste MESSAGE 1, then send each of MESSAGE 2-5
one at a time in the same chat.

## MESSAGE 1 (attach the 3 screenshots)

These are screenshots of my mobile dark-fantasy action RPG (a Diablo-like) in its first
dungeon, a crypt. I want concept art for a redesign of this dungeon that I can rebuild
piece by piece in the game, so it must look like a real in-game screenshot, not an illustration.

Rules for every image in this chat:
- Same camera: true isometric (2:1 diamond floor tiles, no perspective, no vanishing points),
  the same zoom as my screenshots. One floor tile is about as wide as the hero is tall; about
  8 tiles fit across the screen.
- Keep my hero and the skeletons exactly as they look in the screenshots, at the same size
  (about 1/11 of the screen's height).
- Real low-resolution pixel art: as if drawn at 270x420 pixels and scaled up with hard square
  pixels. Limited palette (around 32 colors), 1-pixel dark outlines on objects, flat shading
  in 3-4 tones, dithering allowed, no blur, gradients, glow haze or painterly detail.
- Light from the upper left. Mostly dark: pools of warm light from torches and braziers,
  falling off to black in the corners.
- Walls stand only on the back two sides of a room (upper-left and upper-right). On the near
  sides they are cut down to a low ledge so nothing hides the player.
- No UI: no buttons, bars, text or minimap. Only the game world.
- Portrait, 9:14.

Reply with a short description of how you'll restyle it (walls, floors, colors) before making
any image.

## MESSAGE 2 (image A: the corridor)

Make image A: the corridor screenshot, redesigned. The corridor is 3 to 4 tiles wide, with a
row of square stone pillars down its middle, and it bends once. Its walls have pillars built
into them every 3 tiles, iron torch sconces and a niche holding skulls and candles. It opens
through a carved stone arch into a room. Floor: big worn flagstones, cracks, moss along the
walls, a puddle. The hero walks down it.

Make image B in exactly the same style as image A: the fight screenshot, redesigned as an
ossuary. The back walls are made of stacked skulls and long bones, with a collapsed section and
rubble. There's a cold green fungus glow in one corner against the warm torchlight. The hero
fights four skeletons in the middle.

## MESSAGE 4 (image C: the chapel)

Make image C in exactly the same style: the great hall, redesigned as a ruined chapel. A deep
red carpet runner leads up to an altar with a tall iron candle stand. There are broken pews in
rows, red banners on the walls and a mosaic circle inlaid in the floor. Pale blue moonlight
falls through a crack in the ceiling, with dust floating in it. The hero stands on the carpet.
