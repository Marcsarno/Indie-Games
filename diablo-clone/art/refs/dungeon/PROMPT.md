# Crypt face-lift: prompt for concept art (round 68)

Attach the three screenshots in `current/` (crypt-corridor.png, crypt-fight.png, crypt-hall.png),
then paste the prompt below. Ask for one image at a time, using the four requests at the bottom.

---

These are screenshots from my mobile action RPG, "Dungeons of Bones", a dark-fantasy
Diablo-like in pixel art. This is the first dungeon, an old crypt under a mine. I want a
redesign of the dungeon's look that I can rebuild in the game.

KEEP EXACTLY:
- The camera: the same isometric view (2:1 diamond floor tiles, about 3/4 top-down), the
  same zoom. The hero should be the same size on screen as in my screenshot (a small figure
  about 1/20 of the screen's height).
- Pixel art, crisp, no blur or painterly brushwork. Chunky, readable shapes; every object
  should read at phone size.
- Walls only on the back two sides of a room (upper-left and upper-right edges), cut down low
  on the near sides so the player is never hidden.
- Dark and moody: the room is lit mostly by torches and braziers, and corners fall off to black.
- Portrait phone framing (tall, 9:16). Leave the bottom fifth fairly plain, because my buttons sit there.

CHANGE:
1. Corridors much wider: at least 3 hero-widths across, the main ones 4, sometimes with a row
   of pillars down the middle. Corridors should bend and widen into small alcoves, not run as
   thin straight tubes.
2. Real entrances: a stone arch or carved door frame wherever a corridor opens into a room.
3. Walls with structure, not flat brick: pillars built into the wall every few tiles, varied
   heights, a collapsed section with rubble, niches holding skulls or candles, a carved relief
   or two, iron sconces.
4. Floors with character: big worn flagstones, a mosaic or inlaid circle in big halls, a
   carpet runner leading to an altar, cracks, moss and puddles near the walls, cobwebs in the corners.
5. Rooms with a purpose, each instantly recognizable: an ossuary (walls of stacked skulls and
   bones), a chapel (altar, broken pews, a tall candle stand), a burial hall (rows of stone
   coffins, a few lids pushed off), a prison (cell bars, chains, a rack), a flooded cistern
   (dark water with stepping stones), a library (rotting shelves, scattered pages).
6. More color accents against the brown-and-amber stone: cold green light from fungus or a
   lantern, deep red banners, pale blue moonlight through a crack in the ceiling with dust
   floating in it, warm candle clusters.

Please make:
A) The corridor screenshot, redesigned: a wide corridor with pillars opening through an arch into a room.
B) The fight screenshot, redesigned as the ossuary or the burial hall, with the hero and skeletons fighting.
C) The great hall, redesigned as the chapel, with the carpet runner, altar and light shaft.
D) A reference sheet on a flat dark background: one example of each room type's key props,
   wall pieces (plain wall, wall with a built-in pillar, niche, arch, collapsed section) and
   floor tiles (flagstone, mosaic, cracked, mossy, carpet), all in the same isometric angle and pixel scale.
