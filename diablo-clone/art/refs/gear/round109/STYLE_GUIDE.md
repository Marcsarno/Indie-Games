# Dungeons of Bones - gear style guide (mid-tier gear, levels 6-10)

Reference sheets (same folder): `sheet_overview.png` (house style), `sheet_barbarian.png`,
`sheet_paladin.png`, `sheet_mage.png`, `sheet_ranger.png`. Every figure there is an in-game sprite.

## How the art is made (why these rules exist)
- Heroes are ~64 px tall pixel sprites ray-traced from a rig of simple 3D shapes: spheres,
  ellipsoids, cones, cylinders, boxes. Every gear piece must be describable as 1-6 such shapes.
- Fixed 30-degree isometric camera, 8 facings from one rig: a design must work from front, side, back.
- Shading: 3-5 flat color steps per material, hard cast shadows, a 1 px black outline, one small
  specular glint on metal. Light always from the UPPER LEFT. No gradients, no textures.

## Proportions (chibi)
- Head (with hat/helm) is about 1/3 of total height; short stubby legs; mitten hands.
- Exaggerate the signature pieces: shoulder pads as big as the head, weapon heads as big as a fist
  or the head, hats/horns/halos that add 25-40% height. Small details vanish; big shapes survive.

## Silhouette first
- Each tier must be recognizable as a solid black shape: change the outline (shoulders, headgear,
  weapon head, cape/mantle), not just the colors. Higher tier = bigger, taller, wider silhouette.
- One dominant headgear shape per set (horns, halo, cone hat, hood peak) - it is what reads at 64 px.

## Color
- 2-3 main colors + 1 accent per outfit. Large flat areas of one color (tabard, robe, fur mantle).
- Accent = gold/brass trim, a glowing gem/orb, or war paint - used on 2-3 spots only.
- Keep values separated: dark piece next to light piece (a dark belt on a light robe), never
  mid-on-mid. Skin and faces stay warm and readable.

## Materials (game ramps, see the strip at the bottom of each sheet)
- Metal: STEEL (cool grey), PLATE (darker armor steel), WPLATE (white enamel), DSTEEL (blackened),
  IRON (dull dark), SLATE (blued steel), GOLD, BRONZE (antique brass), RUST.
- Organic: LEATHER (warm brown), HIDE (grey dead hide), FUR (wolf grey), BOAR (dark brown fur),
  HAIR, BONE (ivory), OLDBONE/HORN (yellowed), WOOD, BARK, CHITIN, STRAW.
- Cloth: RED, WINE, BLUE, NAVY, GREEN, PINE, PURPLE, WHITE, TAN, DARK, SHADOW (slate).
- Glow (flat, unlit): FIRE orange, HOLY gold, ARCANE blue, ICE cyan, MAGIC violet, EYE red/green.
  Glow is for orbs, gems, eyes and runes only - one glowing element per outfit at most two.

## Reads at 64 px / does NOT read
- Reads: big pauldrons, horns, halos, tall hats, hood peaks, capes/mantles, belts, a single big
  emblem (sun disc, skull, leaf), oversized weapon heads, 2-3 color bands, glowing orbs.
- Does NOT read - do not draw: thin filigree, chains thinner than a finger, runes or letters,
  engraved patterns, fine stitching, photoreal textures (fur strands, wood grain, scratches),
  busy plaid/checks, many small buckles, transparent or glowing veils, particle effects.

## Class identity rules
- BARBARIAN: bare skin always shows (arms, chest or belly); leather, fur, bone, horn, rough iron.
  War paint stripes, skull/tooth fetishes, fur mantles, horned or skull helms, open-face only.
  NEVER shields, NEVER full plate or closed knightly helms. Weapons: axes, swords, maces, flails,
  dual one-handers or one huge two-hander; chunky, brutal, a little crude.
- PALADIN: white enamel + gold plate, blue (or white) tabard with ONE big sun emblem, huge rounded
  pauldrons, gold halo/sun-crest at high tier. Hammers and maces with big blocky heads; a big
  kite/tower/round sun shield or a two-handed maul. Clean, noble, symmetrical.
- MAGE: floor-length robes (bell silhouette, no visible legs), tall pointed hats (or deep hoods),
  a fur or cloth collar/mantle, a sash. Staff taller than the mage with a BIG head: orb, gem,
  crescent, cage or crystal, one glow color matching the outfit's element.
- RANGER: hood with a peak (green, navy or dark), leather jerkin, scarf/short mantle, bracers,
  boots, earthy greens and browns (plus frost/night variants). Bow held vertical, as tall as the
  ranger; crossbows allowed; quiver on the back with visible arrow fletching. No heavy plate.

## Tier escalation (levels 6 -> 8 -> 10)
- L6: material upgrade + one new signature shape. L8: bigger silhouette, a second accent material,
  a small glow. L10: the heroic look - largest headgear, mantle or cape, best metal, glowing weapon.
