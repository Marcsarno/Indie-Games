# Dungeons of Bones - image-generator prompts for new mid-tier gear (levels 6, 8, 10)

Attach `sheet_overview.png` (and the class's own sheet, e.g. `sheet_paladin.png`) with every prompt.
Paste the HOUSE STYLE preamble once (or in front of each prompt if the generator has no memory).
The images are concept targets: another pass rebuilds them in code from simple 3D shapes.

---

## HOUSE STYLE preamble (paste once)

> Game concept art sheet for "Dungeons of Bones", a dark-fantasy isometric pixel-art action RPG
> for phones whose characters are built in code from simple 3D primitives (spheres, ellipsoids,
> cones, cylinders, boxes) and rendered as ~64-pixel-tall sprites. Match the attached reference
> sheets: chibi proportions (head about one third of the height, short stubby legs, mitten hands),
> oversized shoulder pads, headgear and weapon heads, chunky rounded shapes, bold readable
> silhouette. Show each outfit as an orthographic turnaround of one figure: FRONT, BACK and a
> 3/4 FRONT view, standing in a neutral pose, plus the weapon(s) laid out flat beside it. Clean
> stylized cel shading with 3-4 flat tones per material, light from the upper left, a dark outline,
> one small highlight on metal. 2-3 main colors plus 1 accent per outfit. Plain flat mid-grey
> background, no scenery, no text, no letters, no runes, no logos, no watermark. No thin filigree,
> no fine engraving, no photoreal textures, no busy patterns, no particle effects. Every piece must
> be simple enough to rebuild from a handful of basic 3D shapes.

Each tier prompt below asks for 3 variations in one image: lay them out side by side, each with
its own front / back / 3/4 turnaround, separated by empty space.
