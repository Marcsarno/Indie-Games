## Our review
- Diggers in the pit concept look like human miners: in the game they're snouted, hunched fallen in iron caps. Keep
  them clearly monsters so they never read as the miners you rescue.
- The town's square stays compact (the close-up's size, ~16x16 tiles); the other houses are scenery around it.
- Blackwake's sign: "DANGER" with the level, as the barrow's.
- Shale Ripper's burrow and Gorath's burrow share one mechanic (code once).
- Both loops rejoin the road east, so there's no walking back through town: kept.
