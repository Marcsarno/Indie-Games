## Our review
- Maelis wears the game's look: the tall charcoal wizard hat with an orange band, charcoal robe with orange trim
  (`../attach/maelis.png`), not a purple hood.
- The two new monsters are close in color (dark brown-grey): Shale Ripper goes slate blue-grey with ivory claws and
  the rust mouth; Cinder Husk stays charcoal with amber cracks and the bright chest.
- The ford's slow-down and the sluice's water/collision swap are new engine pieces, both small (like the fallen tree).
- Rusk reuses the brigand rig (as the Bandit Chief does); his lunge is the brigand attack with a longer wind-up.
