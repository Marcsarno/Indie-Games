# Act II overview from ChatGPT (round 70) and our review

Map: `overview-map.png`. Regions west to east: 1 ASHFORD VALE (lv 7-8, ~8 min), 2 THE CINDERWORKS (8-10, ~10 min),
3 THE BROKEN FLANK (10-12, ~12 min) under MOUNT ASH.

Story: the bell rings; Reeve Mara sends you to the RESCUE PIT (D1, caves + mine rooms, Pit Enforcer = ogre enforcer
miniboss) for Foreman Renn -> Renn opens the HAUL GATE -> Cinderworks, the SILENT WHEEL (pumps stopped) -> Maelis at
SILKSHAFT shows the old king's royal seal (what he took in Act I: it unlocked a buried prison under Mount Ash), escapes
through a service passage and drops an iron gate -> SILKSHAFT (D3, webbed mine caves): Broodmother, cocooned miners, the
sluice handle -> the SOUTH SLUICE drains the flooded road -> Broken Flank, the BROKEN BEACON -> THE BREACH (D5, 2 floors,
mine caves turning into prison masonry and lava): GORATH, THE FURNACE MAW (new boss: claw sweep, ground eruption line;
below half it burrows with a visible trail, two fallen crawl out every second emergence; fixed 12x14 arena, lava at the
edges) -> Maelis's discarded page points to the next act.
Optional: D2 BLACKWAKE TOMB (crypt, level 13+, elite tomb guard, burial ledger, a rare), D4 GLASSVEIN GROTTO (crystal
cave, slimes and sporecaps, crystal salts).
Town: Mara Venn (reeve, by the bell), Renn Pike (foreman, mine office), Tamsin Flint (smith: ore from a quarry detour),
Bram Toll (merchant: lockbox from a brigand cache), Nella Reed (potions: crystal salts from Glassvein), Sister Edda
(keeper of the dead: Blackwake's burial ledger). North arrival gate, east adventure gate.

## Our review (what we asked to change)
1. Blackwake Tomb at level 11-12, not 13+ (the act ends at 12; it should be a stretch at the act's end, like the barrow).
2. The Silent Wheel needs a job: a fight there (a brigand camp robbing the abandoned works, their leader carries
   Bram's lockbox) and the place where Renn's crew's trail leads to Silkshaft.
3. Act II needs its own monsters: two new regular monsters from under the mountain (one that burrows and pops up,
   one ash/fire-themed), used from the Cinderworks on, plus Gorath. Everything else reuses Act I's cast.
4. Region 3 is only the Breach: add one more reason to explore (the Beacon as a lit landmark you relight for a
   reward, or a small optional dungeon).
5. Maelis's page should name GREYMARSH (Act III: a drowned village at night).
6. "Miners reopen Ashford's gates" at the end: the gates were never shut; instead the town comes back to life
   (smoke, people at work, the bell rings once).
Kept: the three regions, the gates that open with the story (Haul Gate, South Sluice), the Broodmother mid-act,
Gorath's fight, the six townspeople and their small quests, flat ground with lava only at the edges.
