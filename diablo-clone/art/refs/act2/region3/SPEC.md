# Act II region 3: THE BROKEN FLANK (ChatGPT, round 70) and our review

Images: `region-map-first.png`, `region-map-corrected.png` (ChatGPT says use the corrected one; see the review),
`beacon.png`, `cinder-yard.png`, `breach.png`, `gorath.png`. The arena plan (`Gorath_Arena_Plan.png`) was not attached.

B4-C5 back to E4; south loop E4-D6-D8-F8-G8. Lava always blocked. Areas: landing D1 (safe); ASH ROAD C2-E3 (lv 10,

prison inventory); BREACH FORECOURT E8-G8 (lv 11-12, burning dead + fallen; chains, ward pillars, claw gouges).
Beacon: take fire at the brazier (a flame on the weapon; fight normally, no timer, lost on death or leaving), light the

THE BREACH: floor 1 THE TORN WORKINGS (mine caves: Rippers; Husk + fallen; burning dead at a breached face; human tool

GORATH (about twice the hero): claw sweep (claw drawn back, ~0.9 s; ~1 s recovery); ground eruption (both claws

fallen crawl in (max 4 adds). Arena 12x14 (A-L x 1-14): entrance F14-G14, Gorath starts F4-G5, rocks D6-E7 and H9-I10,
deep lava A3-A12 and L3-L12, crawl-ins C1-D1 and I1-J1, prison wall elsewhere; emergence only on a free spot; the door
closes behind you, opens on victory. After: adds crumble, loot, Maelis's page by the binding pedestal names GREYMARSH;
Ashford smokes, the wheel turns, the bell rings once.

## Our review
- The two maps disagree with the spec: the corrected one adds a mine mouth at D6 (the spec says the sorting works has
  no dungeon) and a shut portcullis on the Breach (the spec says it's already broken open). Build from the spec: D6 as
  a roofless shed (first map), the Breach open (first map, and the close-up).
- No journal in the game: the wards and the page speak in one short line each (like the King's letter).
- Gorath shares the Cinder Husk's materials; keep him apart with the horns, the rust-iron jaw plate, ivory claws,
  darker charcoal hide and fewer, larger fissures. His burrow mound is the Shale Ripper's tell on purpose (players
  learn it from the Rippers first).
