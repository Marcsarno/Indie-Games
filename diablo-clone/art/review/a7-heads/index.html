Smaller hero heads A7

The hero's head was drawn 1.5x its natural size, with every helm, hat, hood and hairdo on it. It's now 1.2x, and everything on the head shrinks with it. The wizard hats and the hoods were then trimmed further to fit the smaller head. All three classes, the townsfolk, the Brigand and the Bandit Chief share this body. Every other monster is unchanged.
