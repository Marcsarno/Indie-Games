Hero armor sets A6

Your six hero concept sheets built into the game: 11 new warrior sets, 4 new ranger sets with the 5 jerkins refined, 4 new wizard sets with the 3 robes refined. Every piece was judged pass by pass against your concept in both left/right orders. The new sets are looks only for now: they need item entries before they can drop. Every other look and every monster renders exactly as before, and no frame clips.
