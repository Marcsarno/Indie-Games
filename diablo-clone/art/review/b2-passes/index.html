Another pass on every monster, plus seven new ones B2

The Brigand Mastiff is gone, as you asked. All 39 remaining monsters got at least 3 more passes, each judged side by side against the best so far in both left/right orders and kept only if it won both. Seven new monsters were built from your concepts (top of the page); they don't spawn yet. Each strip shows before (top) and after, then the silhouette. Nothing else changed: the player heroes and every monster outside each group render exactly as before.
