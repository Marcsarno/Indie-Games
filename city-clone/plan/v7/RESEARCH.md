# What makes a driving map fun (round 37 research) and how plan v7 uses it

Sources were mostly reviews, retrospectives and design talks; no first-hand Rockstar road-design postmortem turned up.

| # | Principle | Where it comes from | In plan v7 (draft 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every district looks and plays differently | Lynch "Image of the City" (paths, edges, districts, nodes, landmarks), used for level design in Brian Upton's GDC talk; Vice City's named districts | Walmart/441 retail, casino + farm fields, Sample car dealers, civic Copans, Tradewinds/Butterfly World park, Wynmoore gated golf, lake neighbourhoods, north construction + offices |
| 2 | Landmarks you can steer by without the map | Upton/Lynch "weenies"; open-world map articles | Casino tower, Butterfly World dome, City Hall clock, Wynmoore water tower, Walmart sign (stars) |
| 3 | Mix of long straights and curves, not all right angles | Forza Horizon designers (favourite road = "a nice collection of straights and turns"); player complaints about only 90-degree corners; hybrid grid + diagonal advice | Straight arterials (real CC grid) + curving Winston Park, the diagonal Sawgrass, the diagonal dirt track, curvy neighbourhood streets |
| 4 | Shortcuts and alternate routes reward knowing the city | Burnout Paradise ("the difficulty of each race is: what is the best route?"), Chinatown Wars (off-road through parks and back alleys), Crazy Taxi | Walmart lot, back lanes behind the shop strips, park trail, canal wading, golf-cart back gate; 3 real Publix-to-Dave routes |
| 5 | Jumps and a bit of height | Burnout (jumps, parking structures), Crazy Taxi 2 (level design built around the jump) | 6 ramps (J): canal banks, Walmart loading dock, farm hay bales, construction site, golf mound; raised Sawgrass |
| 6 | Pursuit breakers and hiding spots | NFS Most Wanted (pursuit breakers + hiding spots) | P: gas pumps, fruit stand, scaffolding, Butterfly World sign, Publix cart corral. H: car wash, parking garage, under the overpass, Walmart garden centre |
| 7 | Choke points create tension; edges frame the city | Vice City's limited bridges; Lynch edges | Canal bridges, Wynmoore gate, edge roadblocks past the shop strips |
| 8 | No empty space; something every few seconds | "Empty world syndrome" (open-world pacing articles) | Every block has a job; at chase speed (~25 m/s) a block passes in ~4 s |
